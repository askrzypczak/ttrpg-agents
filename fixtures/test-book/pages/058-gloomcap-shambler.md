# Gloomcap Shambler

A heap of pale fungus shuffles along the cave floor, trailing a smell of wet stone. Where it has walked, mushrooms the color of old bruises sprout from the soil.

_medium plant, unaligned_

**Armor Class** 11 (natural armor)

**Hit Points** 26 (4d8 + 8)

**Speed** 20 ft.

| STR     | DEX    | CON     | INT    | WIS     | CHA    |
| ------- | ------ | ------- | ------ | ------- | ------ |
| 14 (+2) | 8 (-1) | 14 (+2) | 3 (-4) | 10 (+0) | 5 (-3) |

**Condition Immunities** blinded, deafened

**Senses** blindsight 30 ft. (blind beyond this radius), passive Perception 10

**Languages** -

**Challenge** 1/2 (100 XP)

### Actions

_**Slam.**_ _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 9 (2d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage.

_**Spore Puff.**_ Each creature within 10 feet of the shambler must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of its next turn.

_The Invented Bestiary, page 58_
