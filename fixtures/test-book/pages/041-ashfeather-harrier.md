# Ashfeather Harrier

Gray feathers drift down through the smoke above the burned fields, and the bird that sheds them hunts by sound. Those who see its shadow in the ash rarely see it twice.

_Medium Monstrosity, Unaligned_

**AC** 13 **Initiative** +3 (10+3)

**HP** 22 (4d8 + 4)

**Speed** 10 ft., Fly 60 ft.

### Traits

_**Ash Veil.**_ While the harrier is in smoke or falling ash, attack rolls against it have Disadvantage.

### Actions

_**Talons.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +5, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 9 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage.

_**Ash Shriek (1/Day).**_ _Wisdom Saving Throw:_ DC 12, each creature in a 20-foot Emanation originating from the harrier. _Failure:_ The target has the Frightened condition until the end of its next turn.
