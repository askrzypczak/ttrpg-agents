# Mirewick Lurker

Reeds bend over the black water of the mire, and something patient waits beneath them. Travelers who hear a wet cough from the reeds are already too close.

_Medium Monstrosity, Unaligned_

**AC** 14 **Initiative** +3 (13)

**HP** 33 (6d8 + 6)

**Speed** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.

**Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +5

**Languages** None

### Traits

_**Bog Camouflage.**_ The lurker has Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made in swamps and marshes.

### Actions

_**Multiattack.**_ The lurker makes two Claw attacks.

_**Claw.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +5, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 8 (2d4 + 3) Slashing damage.
