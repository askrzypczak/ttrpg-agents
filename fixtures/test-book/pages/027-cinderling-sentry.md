# Cinderling Sentry

A cinder-colored figure stands guard at the furnace gate, unmoved by heat or hurry. It speaks only when spoken to, and then only in the old tongue.

_Small Elemental, Neutral_

**AC** 15 **Initiative** +1 (10+1)

**HP** 65 (10d6 + 30)

**Speed** 30 ft.

**Languages** Primordial

### Traits

_**Smoldering Body.**_ A creature that touches the sentry or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 5 (2d4) Fire damage.

### Actions

_**Slam.**_ _Melee Attack Roll:_ +4, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 9 (2d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage plus 5 (2d4) Fire damage.

_**Cinder Breath (Recharge 5-6).**_ _Dexterity Saving Throw:_ DC 13, each creature in a 15-foot Cone. _Failure:_ 21 (6d6) Fire damage. _Success:_ Half damage.
